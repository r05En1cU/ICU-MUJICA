## 我给你一个“更收敛版本”的定义（建议你暂时用这个）

如果你现在要把它压成一个 research object，我建议先这样写：

```text
We maintain a Skill Graph as long-term reusable programs.

During multi-step execution of an HDL task, we record execution traces.

We extract repeated or newly useful substructures from traces,
and convert them into incremental graph updates (Addition Graph).

The system continuously compresses execution traces into reusable skills.
```
